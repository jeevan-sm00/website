Welcome to sapthagiri online canteen

This website is created, because of the crowd in the canteen which make the students uneasy to order food. through this website we wanted to make it easier for students to order food and save their time. This website provides services such as selecting the food, placing an order, paying for the food, tracking your order where the staff is directly notified of your order and are preparing it. Once the order is ready it is notified to you,


This website contains four section where in the home section you can browse through the trending food items and get notified of the future updates, you can also contact us on this page regarding any issues. in the food page you can see all the items which are provided by the canteen along with the food name and thier price, here you can add the food you want and enter the quantity of serving, after selecting the food and quantity, you can go to the cart section where you can pay for the food and track your food in tracking page which shows if the order is being prepared and is it ready
